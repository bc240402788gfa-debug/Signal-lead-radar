How scoring works

Each score starts from a base value for the business category — marketing agencies and logistics score higher than a generic office, for example — then adds points for a listed website, phone number, street address, and chain/brand tag: signals that a lead is real, reachable, and has some scale. There's no revenue, employee count, or review data behind this; free map data doesn't include it. Coverage also depends on how well an area is mapped — expect more results in major cities than small towns. Treat the ranking as a shortlist to investigate, not a verdict.
