How scoring works

Each score starts from a base value for the business category — visually-driven, consumer-facing businesses like restaurants, salons, and hotels score higher than back-office B2B categories, since they're the easiest social media pitch to make. Points are added for an existing Facebook or Instagram presence — a business already active on social (even if posting inconsistently) is a hotter lead than one with no presence at all, since they've already shown they see the value. A listed phone number and street address add a little more, since they're signals the business is easy to reach and reasonably established. There's no follower count, engagement rate, or revenue data behind this; free map data doesn't include it. Coverage also depends on how well an area is mapped — expect more results in major cities than small towns. Treat the ranking as a shortlist to investigate, not a verdict.
